Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account (optional): your email address and a password (stored hashed by our authentication provider).
  • Your lists: list names, items, quantities and sharing codes. For signed-in users these are synced to our servers; for guests they stay on your device.
  • Anonymous ID: every user, including guests, gets an anonymous account with a random ID and no email. It is used to limit daily AI usage. Lists of guests are not uploaded.
  • AI usage counts: how many AI requests your account made today.
  • Content you send to AI features: typed prompts and photos you choose to scan.
  • Voice input: if you use voice entry, the audio is processed by your device’s speech recognition service (on Android, typically Google). We do not receive or store the audio.

How we use it

Only to run Shoppy: sign-in, syncing and sharing lists, and generating AI suggestions. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it for tracking.

Third-party services

  • Supabase – authentication, database and server functions (supabase.com/privacy).
  • Google (Gemini API) – when you use Smart Suggestions or Scan Photo, your prompt or photo is sent to Google to produce the result (policies.google.com/privacy). We do not store your photos.
  • Your device’s speech recognition service – for voice input, as described above.

Sharing

If you share a list using its code, anyone who joins it can see and edit its contents and see that it is shared with them. Only share codes with people you trust.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account data until you delete your account. You can delete it at any time in the app under Settings → Delete Account, which permanently removes your account and all your lists from our servers, including lists you own that were shared with others. Lists already stored on your device stay on your device. See how to delete your account, including if you no longer have the app.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to each list is restricted to its owner and the people it is shared with.

Children

Shoppy is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
