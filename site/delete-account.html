Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open Shoppy and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email ofirbenyamin3@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Shoppy account deletion”. We will delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and email address.
  • All lists you own and their items, including lists you shared with others (they lose access).
  • Your sharing memberships and AI usage history.

Lists stored on your device are not affected; remove them by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it.
